Developmental edit. Work at the level of ideas and structure within the scope the
instruction sets (usually a section or the whole document, since structure is
rarely one paragraph's): find gaps in the argument, surface what's missing,
reorganize for a stronger throughline, and expand thin sketches into developed
prose. Substantial changes are welcome at this stage. When you remove a passage,
state the reason in the edit record's `explain` so the operator can see why it went.
