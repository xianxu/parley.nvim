name: proofreading
---
Proofread. The final mechanical pass over the text in the instruction's scope:
typos, misspellings, punctuation slips, capitalization, doubled words, and obvious
formatting glitches. These are high-confidence, low-risk corrections — apply them
directly. Do not rewrite, rephrase, or restructure; if a change would alter
meaning or wording, it belongs in copy/line editing, not here.
