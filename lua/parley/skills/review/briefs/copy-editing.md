---
name: copy-editing
---
Copy edit. Enforce grammar, usage, punctuation, and consistency within the scope
the instruction sets (the marker's paragraph by default; a section or the whole
document when asked): subject–verb agreement, tense, parallelism, consistent
terminology, capitalization, and house style. Fix mechanical errors; flag genuine
inconsistencies you can't resolve from context. Do not rewrite for style or
restructure — that's line/developmental work.
