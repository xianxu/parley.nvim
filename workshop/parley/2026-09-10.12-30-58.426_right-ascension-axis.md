The confusing part is that **“the direction something points” and “the axis it rotates around” are different things.** My earlier wording didn’t make that distinction clear.

### Imagine photographing from a swivel chair

Sit in a swivel chair and aim your camera horizontally at something:

- The chair’s **rotation axis is vertical**—toward the ceiling.
- Your camera **points horizontally**—toward the wall.
- Turn the chair, and the camera sweeps around the room, even though it never points toward the ceiling.

**Your mount works the same way**, except its rotation axis is tilted toward the celestial pole rather than straight up.

### Why this follows M31

As Earth rotates, M31 appears to move **along a circle around the celestial pole**. Polaris is near the center of that apparent rotation—not the place you need to photograph.

To follow M31:

1. **Polar-align:** Set the mount’s RA axis parallel to Earth’s rotation axis.
2. **Aim:** Use the RA and DEC rotations to point the camera at M31.
3. **Track:** The RA motor slowly rotates the camera **around that aligned axis**, matching M31’s apparent movement across the sky.

Turning around an axis **doesn’t change where the axis itself points**—just as spinning a chair doesn’t tilt its central shaft.

So there’s no contradiction: **the RA axis points toward the center of the sky’s apparent rotation; the camera points at an object moving around that center.**

The adjustment marked **latitude** is called the **altitude adjustment**, or **latitude adjustment**. It tilts the entire RA axis up or down relative to the horizon.

**You’re right that it’s another rotational freedom—but RA, DEC, and latitude aren’t a set of three independent spatial directions.** They are mechanical adjustments stacked on one another. Your mount actually has **four relevant rotations**:

| Adjustment | What it changes | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Altitude / latitude** | Tilts the RA axis up or down | Polar alignment |
| **Azimuth** | Turns the mount’s base left or right | Polar alignment |
| **RA** | Rotates the camera assembly around the polar-aligned axis | Aiming and tracking |
| **DEC** | Rotates the camera around an axis perpendicular to RA | Aiming |

Think of it as **two adjustments to orient the mount, then two to aim the camera**:

- **Altitude + azimuth:** Point the **RA axis** toward the celestial pole. Leave these fixed after polar alignment.
- **RA + DEC:** Point the **lens** at a target, without changing where the RA axis points.

Also, the precise term is **axis of rotation**, not “plane.”
