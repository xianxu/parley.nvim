**Yes—you need a ¼″-20 camera mounting screw** to attach the lens’s tripod foot to a dovetail plate. Your GTi accepts a **Vixen-style dovetail plate**, which then clamps into the mount’s saddle. See the [GTi mounting manual](https://inter-static.skywatcher.com/downloads/sagti_manual_rev8c.pdf). ([inter-static.skywatcher.com](https://inter-static.skywatcher.com/downloads/sagti_manual_rev8c.pdf?utm_source=openai))

The arrangement should be:

**Lens tripod foot → mounting screw through dovetail plate → GTi saddle**

For your 100–500mm, use the **lens’s tripod collar/foot**, rather than attaching the R5 body to the plate; Canon provides that collar for tripod mounting. ([usa.canon.com](https://www.usa.canon.com/support/p/rf100-500mm-f4-5-7-1-l-is-usm?utm_source=openai))

**The screw’s length depends on your plate**, so don’t buy an arbitrary long bolt: it needs to tighten the foot against the plate without bottoming out inside the threaded socket.

**Do you already have a dovetail plate?** Send a photo or its product name—ideally showing the underside and mounting slot—and I can help identify the appropriate screw and whether any adapter is needed.
